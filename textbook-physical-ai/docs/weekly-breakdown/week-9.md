# Week 9: Human-Robot Interaction and Social Robotics

## Overview
This week explores human-robot interaction (HRI) and social robotics, focusing on how humanoid robots can interact naturally and safely with humans in shared environments. Students will learn about social cues, communication modalities, and the design principles for effective human-robot collaboration.

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this week, students will be able to:
- Understand the principles of human-robot interaction
- Implement multimodal communication systems
- Design socially appropriate robot behaviors
- Evaluate human-robot interaction quality
- Consider ethical implications of social robotics
