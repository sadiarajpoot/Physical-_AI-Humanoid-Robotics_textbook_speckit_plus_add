# Week 1: Introduction to Physical AI

## Overview
This week introduces the fundamental concepts of Physical AI and sets the foundation for the entire course. Students will explore the differences between traditional digital AI and Physical AI, understand the importance of embodiment in intelligence, and begin familiarizing themselves with the course tools and platforms.

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this week, students will be able to:
- Define Physical AI and distinguish it from traditional digital AI
- Explain the importance of embodiment in intelligent systems
- Identify key challenges and opportunities in Physical AI
- Set up the development environment for the course
- Understand the course structure and expectations
