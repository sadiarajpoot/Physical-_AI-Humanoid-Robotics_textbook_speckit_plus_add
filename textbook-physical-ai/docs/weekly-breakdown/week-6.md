# Week 6: Humanoid Kinematics and Dynamics

## Overview
This week focuses on the mathematical foundations for understanding and controlling humanoid robot movement. Students will learn kinematics (the study of motion without considering forces) and dynamics (the study of motion with forces) as they apply to humanoid robots.

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this week, students will be able to:
- Understand forward and inverse kinematics for humanoid robots
- Apply homogeneous transformation matrices for pose representation
- Analyze the dynamics of multi-body systems
- Implement kinematic and dynamic models for humanoid robots
- Design control strategies based on kinematic and dynamic models
