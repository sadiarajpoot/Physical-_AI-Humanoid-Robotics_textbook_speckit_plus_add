# Week 7: Humanoid Locomotion and Walking

## Overview
This week explores the complex challenge of humanoid locomotion, focusing on how robots achieve stable and efficient bipedal movement. Students will learn about walking pattern generation, balance control, and the biomechanics-inspired approaches to humanoid locomotion.

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this week, students will be able to:
- Understand the biomechanics of human walking
- Implement walking pattern generation algorithms
- Design balance control systems for bipedal robots
- Analyze and optimize walking stability
- Integrate locomotion with higher-level planning

## Day 1: Introduction to Humanoid Locomotion
### Topics Covered
- Biomechanics of human walking
- Differences between wheeled and legged locomotion
- Challenges in bipedal walking
- Stability concepts in walking

### Activities
- Analyzing human walking patterns
- Comparing different locomotion approaches
- Understanding stability requirements

### Readings
- Humanoid locomotion research papers
- Biomechanics of walking literature

## Day 2: Walking Pattern Generation
### Topics Covered
- Footstep planning algorithms
- Trajectory generation for walking
- Center of mass motion planning
- Swing and stance phase planning

### Activities
- Implementing footstep planners
- Generating walking trajectories
- Visualizing walking patterns

### Readings
- Walking pattern generation techniques
- Footstep planning algorithms

## Day 3: Balance Control and Stability
### Topics Covered
- Zero moment point (ZMP) theory
- Capture point and viability
- Feedback control for balance
- Disturbance rejection

### Activities
- Computing ZMP for walking patterns
- Implementing balance feedback controllers
- Testing stability in simulation

### Readings
- Balance control theory
- ZMP-based walking control

## Day 4: Advanced Walking Techniques
### Topics Covered
- Walking on uneven terrain
- Turning and direction changes
- Walking speed modulation
- Energy-efficient walking

### Activities
- Implementing terrain-adaptive walking
- Creating turning and speed control
- Optimizing energy efficiency

### Readings
- Advanced walking control techniques

## Day 5: Integration and Optimization
### Topics Covered
- Integration with perception systems
- Real-time walking control
- Learning-based walking adaptation
- Safety considerations in walking

### Activities

- Testing complete walking system
- Analyzing walking performance

### Assignments
