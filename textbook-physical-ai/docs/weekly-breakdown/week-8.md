# Week 8: Humanoid Manipulation and Dexterous Control

## Overview
This week focuses on humanoid manipulation capabilities, exploring how robots can perform complex tasks with their arms and hands. Students will learn about grasp planning, dexterous manipulation, and the integration of manipulation with whole-body control.

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this week, students will be able to:
- Understand the principles of robotic manipulation and grasping
- Implement grasp planning and execution algorithms
- Design dexterous manipulation controllers
- Integrate manipulation with locomotion and balance
- Evaluate manipulation performance and robustness

- Manipulation challenges for humanoid robots
- Human-inspired manipulation strategies
