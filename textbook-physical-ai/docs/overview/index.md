# Quarter Overview

Welcome to the comprehensive textbook on Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics. This quarter-long course is designed to provide you with a deep understanding of the principles and applications of physical artificial intelligence in humanoid robotics.

## Course Structure

This course is divided into four main modules, each focusing on a critical aspect of physical AI and humanoid robotics:

1. **Module 1: The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2)** - Understanding the communication and coordination framework for robotics
2. **Module 2: The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)** - Creating virtual environments for robot simulation and testing
3. **Module 3: The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac™)** - Implementing intelligent control systems for humanoid robots
4. **Module 4: Vision-Language-Action (VLA)** - Integrating perception, reasoning, and action in robotic systems

## Learning Approach

This course takes a hands-on approach to learning, combining theoretical foundations with practical implementation. You will work with state-of-the-art tools and frameworks while developing real-world applications in humanoid robotics.

## Prerequisites

Before starting this course, students should have:
- Basic programming experience (Python preferred)
- Understanding of linear algebra and calculus
- Familiarity with robotics concepts (optional but recommended)
